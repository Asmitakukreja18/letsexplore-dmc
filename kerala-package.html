7 Days Detailed Itinerary

Day-by-Day Experience

Day 1 · 29 Sep 2026
Cochin to Munnar
140 KM · 4 Hours Scenic Drive

Arrive at Cochin, the gateway of Kerala. Our dedicated AC cab and driver will meet & greet you at the airport/station.

Proceed to Munnar through the lush Neriyamangalam Forest. On the way, visit Valara Waterfalls, Cheeyappara Waterfalls, and sprawling tea plantations. Check into Lake N Hills Resort for an overnight stay in Munnar.

Day 2 · 30 Sep 2026
Munnar Full Day Sightseeing
Tea Gardens & Anaimudi Peak

After breakfast, enjoy full-day Munnar local sightseeing.

Visit Photo Point, Mattupetty Dam & boating point, Elephant Arrival Spot, and Echo Point. After lunch, visit Tata Tea Museum, Anaimudi View (Rajamala / Eravikulam National Park — home to Nilgiri Tahr and South India’s highest peak at 8,841 ft), Blossom Park, Pothamedu Sunset Viewpoint, and Cardamom, Coffee & Pepper plantations. Overnight in Munnar.

Day 3 · 01 Oct 2026
Munnar to Thekkady
Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary

After breakfast, check out and drive to Thekkady (Periyar).

Visit Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary where you can spot wild elephants, boars, deer, and exotic birdlife. Tour the Mullaperiyar Dam surroundings and spice plantations for authentic Kerala spices. In the evening, enjoy a boat cruise on Periyar Lake. Check in to Sandra Palace for overnight stay.

Day 4 · 02 Oct 2026
Thekkady to Alleppey
Backwaters & Punnamada Lake

After breakfast, drive to Alleppey, Venice of the East.

Sightseeing includes Alleppey Backwaters, Beach, Historic Light House, Revi Karunakaran Museum, Sri Rajarajeswari Mullakkal Temple, and Punnamada Lake. Enjoy a serene village walk through coconut groves. Overnight stay at Resort Alleppey.

Day 5 · 03 Oct 2026
Alleppey to Kovalam
Coastal Beach Resort Arrival

After breakfast, check out and drive towards Kovalam.

Enjoy the scenic coastal drive. Check into Golden Sand Beach Resort, Kovalam. Relax by the Arabian Sea beach shacks and enjoy an evening sunset stroll.

Day 6 · 04 Oct 2026
Kovalam & Poovar Island
Trivandrum City & Padmanabhaswamy

After breakfast, visit Poovar Beach & mangrove estuary, then drive to Trivandrum (capital city).

Visit the iconic Padmanabhaswamy Temple (Darshan Timings: Morning 3:15AM–12:00PM, Evening 5:00PM–6:15PM). Tour Napier Museum, Trivandrum Zoo, and Sree Chitra Art Gallery. Overnight stay at Golden Sand Beach Resort, Kovalam.

Day 7 · 05 Oct 2026
Departure from Kerala
Airport Transfer

After breakfast, check out from resort with fond memories of Kerala.

Private vehicle transfer to Trivandrum / Cochin Airport for your return flight home.
