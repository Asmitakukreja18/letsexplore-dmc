10 Days Detailed Itinerary

Day-by-Day Island Hopping Experience

Day 1 · 28 Aug 2026
Arrival in Phuket
Patong Beach Welcome

Arrive at Phuket International Airport. Private transfer to Casa Del M Phuket (4★ Premium) – Superior Room.

Check-in and relax. Free time in the evening to explore Patong Beach, Bangla Road nightlife, or local night markets. Overnight stay in Phuket.

Day 2 · 29 Aug 2026
James Bond Island Tour
Phang Nga Bay Cruise

Breakfast at the hotel. Pickup by private transfer to the pier.

Embark on the James Bond Island Tour by Long Tail Boat (SIC). Tour includes Phang Nga Bay sea canoeing, Koh Panyee floating village, buffet lunch, and National Park Fees. Return to hotel by private transfer. Overnight stay in Phuket.

Day 3 · 30 Aug 2026
Phi Phi Island Tour
Speedboat Snorkeling & Maya Bay

Breakfast at the hotel. Private transfer to the pier.

Board high-speed speedboat for Phi Phi Island Tour (SIC). Explore Maya Bay, Pileh Lagoon, Viking Cave, and Monkey Beach. Enjoy snorkeling among tropical fish. Lunch and National Park fees included. Return to hotel. Overnight stay in Phuket.

Day 4 · 31 Aug 2026
Simon Cabaret Show
Spectacular Evening Performance

Breakfast at the hotel. Morning and afternoon at leisure for shopping or beach relaxing.

In the evening, enjoy the famous Simon Cabaret Show featuring glamorous dance performances, extravagant costumes, and state-of-the-art sound. Private transfers included. Overnight stay in Phuket.

Day 5 · 01 Sep 2026
Phuket to Krabi
Ao Nang Beach Resort Arrival

Breakfast and check-out from Phuket hotel.

Private transfer to The Cliff Elegance Resort, Krabi (4★ Premium). Check-in and relax. Enjoy free evening walking along Ao Nang Beach with stunning limestone cliff views. Overnight stay in Krabi.

Day 6 · 02 Sep 2026
Krabi 4 Island Tour
Koh Poda, Chicken Island & Phra Nang

Breakfast at hotel. Private transfer to the pier.

Embark on 4 Island Tour by Long Tail Boat (SIC). Visit Koh Poda, Chicken Island, Tup Island, and Phra Nang Cave Beach. Buffet lunch and National Park Fee included. Return to hotel. Overnight stay in Krabi.

Day 7 · 03 Sep 2026
Leisure in Krabi
Optional Emerald Pool & Hot Springs

Breakfast at the hotel. Enjoy the entire day at leisure.

Optional activities: Visit Railay Beach, Emerald Pool & Krabi Hot Springs, Tiger Cave Temple, or pamper yourself with an authentic Thai massage. Overnight stay in Krabi.

Day 8 · 04 Sep 2026
Krabi to Koh Samui
Chaweng Noi Pool Villa Check-in

Breakfast and check-out. Transfer to Koh Samui island paradise.

Private transfer from Koh Samui Airport / Ferry Pier to Chaweng Noi Pool Villa (4★ Premium) – Deluxe Room / Villa. Check-in, unwind by the pool, and enjoy Koh Samui beach atmosphere. Overnight stay in Koh Samui.

Day 9 · 05 Sep 2026
Angthong Marine Park Tour
Speedboat Kayaking & Emerald Lagoon

Breakfast at hotel. Private transfer to the pier.

Full Day Angthong Marine National Park Tour by Speed Boat. Experience ocean sea kayaking, climbing up to Green Lagoon viewpoint, snorkeling, lunch, and National Park Fee included. Return to hotel by private transfer. Overnight stay in Koh Samui.

Day 10 · 06 Sep 2026
Departure from Thailand
Airport Transfer

Breakfast at the hotel. Check-out.

Private transfer to Koh Samui Airport for flight departure with wonderful memories of your Thailand island hopping trip.
