5 Days Detailed Itinerary

Day-by-Day Experience

Day 1 · 15 May 2026
Port Blair Arrival & Cellular Jail
Corbyn's Cove & Light & Sound Show

Arrival at Port Blair Veer Savarkar International Airport (IXZ). Private AC vehicle transfer to hotel.

Visit Corbyn's Cove Beach for coconut water & coastal views. Visit historical Cellular Jail Museum. In the evening, watch the inspiring Light & Sound Show at Cellular Jail showcasing India's freedom struggle. Overnight stay in Port Blair.

Day 2 · 16 May 2026
Pvt Ferry to Havelock Island
Sunset at Radhanagar Beach

Breakfast at hotel. Board high-speed Private Cruise Ferry (Makruzz / Nautika) to Havelock Island.

Arrive at Havelock jetty and transfer to beach resort. Afternoon visit to Asia's best beach — Radhanagar Beach (Beach No. 7) for white sand walk and mesmerising sunset over the Andaman Sea. Overnight stay in Havelock Island.

Day 3 · 17 May 2026
Pvt Ferry to Neil Island
Bharatpur, Laxmanpur & Coral Bridge

Breakfast at resort. Board private ferry transfer from Havelock to Neil Island.

Check-in at Neil Island resort. Visit Bharatpur Beach (crystal clear water & water sports), Laxmanpur Beach (spectacular sunset views), and famous Natural Rock Formation (Howrah Coral Bridge). Overnight stay in Neil Island.

Day 4 · 18 May 2026
Pvt Ferry to Port Blair & Shopping
Sagarika Emporium Handicraft Shopping

Breakfast at resort. Board private cruise ferry back from Neil Island to Port Blair.

Check-in at Port Blair hotel. Afternoon shopping at Sagarika Govt Cottage Industries Emporium for pearl jewelry, wooden artifacts & sea shell handicrafts. Overnight stay in Port Blair.

Day 5 · 19 May 2026
Port Blair Airport Departure
IXZ Airport Drop

Breakfast at hotel. Check-out from hotel.

Private AC vehicle transfer to Veer Savarkar Airport Port Blair for departure flight with tropical island memories.
