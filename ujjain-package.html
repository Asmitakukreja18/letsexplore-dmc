5 Days Detailed Itinerary

Day-by-Day Experience

Day 1 · 26 Sep 2026
Indore/Ujjain Arrival & Mahakal Lok
Mahakaleshwar Sheeghra Darshan

Pickup from Indore Airport/Railway Station or Ujjain Station. Private AC Sedan transfer to Ujjain hotel.

Check-in and refresh. Sheeghra VIP Darshan at Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga, explore Mahakal Lok Corridor, Harsiddhi Temple, Bade Ganesh Temple, Ram Ghat, Sandipani Ashram, Mangalnath, Kal Bhairav Temple, ISKCON & Gopal Mandir. 3D VR Bhasm Aarti 8-min show included. Overnight stay in Ujjain.

Day 2 · 27 Sep 2026
Omkareshwar Jyotirlinga Excursion
Narmada River & Mamleshwar Temple

Breakfast at hotel. Full day excursion to sacred island of Omkareshwar.

Sheeghra VIP Darshan at Omkareshwar Jyotirlinga, visit Mamleshwar Temple across Narmada river, Omkar Parvat view, and Siddhanath Temple. Enjoy serene holy Narmada ghat views. Return to Ujjain in the evening. Overnight stay in Ujjain.

Day 3 · 28 Sep 2026
Ujjain to Indore City Tour
Rajwada, Khajrana & Sarafa Food Market

Breakfast and check-out from Ujjain hotel. Drive to clean city of Indore.

Check-in at Indore hotel. Visit royal Rajwada Palace, Lal Bagh Palace, Kanch Mandir (Glass Temple), Khajrana Ganesh Temple, Annapurna Temple, Chappan Dukan food street, and famous Sarafa Night Food Market. Overnight stay in Indore.

Day 4 · 29 Sep 2026
Maheshwar & Mandu Heritage Excursion
Ahilya Fort, Jahaz Mahal & Roopmati Pavilion

Breakfast at hotel. Full day heritage excursion to Maheshwar & Mandu.

Visit royal Ahilya Fort, Maheshwar Narmada Ghats, Kashi Vishwanath Temple, Ek Mukhi Datta Temple. Proceed to historic Mandu: explore Jahaz Mahal (Ship Palace), Hindola Mahal, Hoshang Shah's Tomb, Jami Masjid, Baz Bahadur's Palace, and romantic Rani Roopmati Pavilion. Return to Indore in evening. Overnight stay in Indore.

Day 5 · 30 Sep 2026
Indore Airport / Station Departure
Onward Journey Transfer

Breakfast at hotel. Check-out from hotel.

Private AC Sedan transfer to Indore Airport / Railway Station for your onward journey with divine blessings of Mahakal & Omkareshwar.
