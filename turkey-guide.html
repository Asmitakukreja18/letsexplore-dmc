Best Time to Visit

Timing is everything. Experience Turkey's diverse climate at its most comfortable and picturesque.

eco

Spring (April – May)

The ideal window for exploring Istanbul and the interior. Tulips bloom throughout the cities, and temperatures are pleasantly mild (15°C to 25°C), making it perfect for archaeological walks in Ephesus.

check_circle Best for Trekking & Sightseeing
sunny_snowing

Autumn (Sept – Oct)

The heat of summer fades but the sea remains warm. This is the prime time for coastal Turkey and the Aegean region. Markets are bustling with harvest, and the light for photography is golden and soft.

Pro Travel Tips

payments

Currency & Payments

Turkish Lira (TRY) is the official currency. Credit cards are widely accepted in cities, but carry cash for local bazaars and remote areas.

mosque

Local Etiquette

When visiting mosques, dress modestly. Remove shoes before entering and women should carry a scarf to cover their hair.

translate

Communication

English is common in tourist zones, but learning basic Turkish phrases like 'Merhaba' (Hello) goes a long way with locals.
