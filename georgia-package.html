Handcrafted Itinerary

Day-by-Day Journey

D1
Tbilisi Arrival & Old Town

Arrival in Tbilisi & Historic Old Town Exploration

Welcome to Georgia! Upon arrival at Tbilisi International Airport, enjoy a VIP greeting and private transfer to your boutique city hotel. After check-in, begin your exploration of Old Tbilisi. Walk along narrow cobblestone streets lined with carved wooden balconies, admire the Bridge of Peace glowing at dusk, and take in the relaxing atmosphere of the historic sulfur bath district (Abanotubani).

Private Airport Transfer Bridge of Peace Abanotubani
D2
Capital Highlights

Tbilisi Scenic Cable Car, Sameba & Culinary Tasting

After breakfast, embark on a full city tour. Ride the aerial cable car from Rike Park over the Mtkvari River up to Narikala Fortress for panoramic views of Tbilisi. Visit Mother of Georgia (Kartlis Deda) statue and Holy Trinity Cathedral (Sameba), one of the largest Orthodox cathedrals in the world. In the afternoon, enjoy a culinary stop to try authentic Georgian Khachapuri (cheese bread) and Khinkali (spiced dumplings).

Rike Park Cable Car Ticket Included Sameba Cathedral Georgian Food Tasting
D3
UNESCO & Alpine Drive

Mtskheta Ancient Capital & Journey to Gudauri Mountains

Depart Tbilisi along the historic Georgian Military Highway. Stop at Mtskheta, Georgia’s ancient capital and UNESCO World Heritage site. Visit Jvari Monastery perched on a cliff edge with breathtaking views of the river confluence, and Svetitskhoveli Cathedral. Continue into the High Caucasus mountains, passing Ananuri Fortress complex overlooking Zhinvali Reservoir, before arriving at the ski resort town of Gudauri.

Mtskheta UNESCO Tour Ananuri Fortress Gudauri Mountain Resort Stay
D4
High Caucasus Peak

Kazbegi Off-Road 4x4 Jeep & Gergeti Trinity Church

Travel further north to Stepantsminda (Kazbegi). Board rugged 4x4 Delica Jeeps to climb up the mountain slopes to Gergeti Trinity Church, sitting dramatically at 2,170 meters elevation against the backdrop of snow-capped Mount Kazbek (5,054m). Capture incredible photos at the Russia-Georgia Friendship Monument panorama deck before returning to Tbilisi for a relaxing evening.

Kazbegi 4x4 Jeep Safari Gergeti Trinity Church Friendship Monument Views
D5
Wine Tasting & Departure

Georgian Wine Tasting & Airport Farewell

Savor your final Georgian breakfast. Spend the morning souvenir shopping at the open-air Dry Bridge Flea Market or enjoy a complimentary Georgian wine tasting session to experience Georgia's 8,000-year-old winemaking heritage. Check out from your hotel and board your private transfer to Tbilisi International Airport for your return flight home.

Georgian Wine Tasting Souvenir Shopping Private Airport Drop-off
