5 Days Detailed Itinerary

Day-by-Day Experience

Day 1 · 15 Apr 2026
Srinagar Arrival & Dal Lake Shikara
Floating Gardens & Kashmiri Kahwa

Arrive at Srinagar Sheikh ul-Alam International Airport (SXR). Private transfer to deluxe hotel in Srinagar.

Check-in and refresh. Afternoon 1-Hour Shikara Ride on Dal Lake exploring floating vegetable gardens, lotus swamps & local handicraft boats. Enjoy hot traditional Kashmiri Kahwa tea. Dinner and overnight stay in Srinagar.

Day 2 · 16 Apr 2026
Day Excursion to Sonmarg
Meadow of Gold & Thajiwas Glacier

Breakfast at hotel. Full day excursion to Sonmarg ("Meadow of Gold", 2,730m altitude).

Scenic 3-hour drive alongside Sindh River & snow-capped Himalayan peaks. Take local union cabs to Zero Point via Zoji La Pass or visit Thajiwas Glacier for snow bike & sledding activities. Return to Srinagar in evening. Dinner and overnight stay in Srinagar.

Day 3 · 17 Apr 2026
Day Excursion to Pahalgam
Aru & Betaab Valley Union Cabs

Breakfast at hotel. Drive to Pahalgam ("Valley of Shepherds", 2,200m altitude).

Enroute visit Pampore Saffron fields, cricket bat factory & 9th-century Avantiswami Temple ruins. Board local union vehicle excursion to Aru Valley & Betaab Valley (famous Bollywood filming location surrounded by pine forests). Return to Srinagar in evening. Dinner and overnight stay in Srinagar.

Day 4 · 18 Apr 2026
Day Excursion to Gulmarg & Gondola
Gondola Cable Car Phase 1 Kongdoori

Breakfast at hotel. Full day excursion to Gulmarg ("Meadow of Flowers", 2,650m altitude).

Scenic drive via Tangmarg pine forests. Board the world-famous Gulmarg Gondola Cable Car Ride (Phase 1 Kongdoori) for panoramic views of Mt. Affarwat snow fields. Return to Srinagar in evening. Dinner and overnight stay in Srinagar.

Day 5 · 19 Apr 2026
Mughal Gardens Tour & Departure
Nishat Bagh, Shalimar & SXR Drop

Breakfast at hotel. Check-out from hotel.

Visit famous Mughal Gardens: Nishat Bagh (Garden of Pleasure) & Shalimar Bagh (Abode of Love). Visit Kashmiri carpet-weaving factory & handicraft shop. Private transfer to Srinagar Airport for departure flight with fond Himalayan memories.
