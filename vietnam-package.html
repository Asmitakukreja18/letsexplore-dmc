12 Days Detailed Itinerary

Day-by-Day Experience

Day 1 · 05 Jun
Hanoi Arrival & Old Quarter
Arrival at Noi Bai Airport. Transfer to hotel. Evening Hoan Kiem Lake & Old Quarter drive. Indian/veg friendly dinner.
Day 2 · 06 Jun
Ha Long Bay Day Cruise
Drive to Ha Long Bay. Join day cruise among limestone karsts, Sung Sot Cave, Titov Island. Cruise lunch & dinner included.
Day 3 · 07 Jun
Ninh Binh & Trang An Boat
Hoa Lu Ancient Capital, Trang An rowing boat ride through river caves & Mua Cave viewpoint. Full board meals.
Day 4 · 08 Jun
Hanoi to Sapa Cat Cat Village
Private bus drive to Sapa mountain resort. Check-in & visit Cat Cat H'Mong minority village. Dinner & stay in Sapa.
Day 5 · 09 Jun
Fansipan Summit & Lao Chai
Fansipan Mountain Cable Car ("Roof of Indochina"), Sapa Stone Church & Lao Chai Ta Van village. Buffet lunch & dinner.
Day 6 · 10 Jun
Sapa to Hanoi Airport -> Fly Da Nang
Drive back to Hanoi airport, domestic flight to coastal Da Nang. Hotel transfer & leisure time. Dinner included.
Day 7 · 11 Jun
Ba Na Hills & Golden Bridge
Cable car to Ba Na Hills, walk on iconic Golden Bridge held by stone hands, French Village & Fantasy Park. Buffet lunch.
Day 8 · 12 Jun
Marble Mountains & Hoi An Ancient Town
Marble Mountains, Lady Buddha at Linh Ung Pagoda, Dragon Bridge. Evening lantern walking tour in Hoi An.
Day 9 · 13 Jun
Da Nang Fly to Phu Quoc Island
Flight to Phu Quoc Island. Visit Ho Quoc Pagoda, Sao Beach & Sunset Town photo stop. Dinner included.
Day 10 · 14 Jun
VinWonders & Grand World Phu Quoc
VinWonders theme park & aquarium, Grand World Venice canal photo stops. Buffet lunch & dinner included.
Day 11 · 15 Jun
Phu Quoc Island Heritage Tour
Visit pearl farm, pepper farm, fish sauce factory, Dinh Cau sea temple & beach leisure. Lunch & dinner included.
Day 12 · 16 Jun
Departure from Phu Quoc
Breakfast, check-out & private 29-seater bus transfer to Phu Quoc Airport for return flight home.
