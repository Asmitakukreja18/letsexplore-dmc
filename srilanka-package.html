5 Days Detailed Itinerary

Day-by-Day Experience

Day 1 · 16 Oct 2026
Airport to Kandy
Pinnawala Elephant Sanctuary

Meeting & greeting at Bandaranaike International Airport by representative. Transfer to Kandy via Pinnawala.

Visit Pinnawala Elephant Orphanage (established 1975, a 24-acre sanctuary home to the world's largest captive herd of wild elephants). Watch elephants bathe in Ma Oya River. Evening Kandyan Cultural Dance & Fire Walking Show (if time permits). Dinner and overnight stay at Kandy City Hotel by Earl's.

Day 2 · 17 Oct 2026
Kandy to Nuwara Eliya
Temple of Tooth, Ramboda & Tea Factory

Breakfast at hotel. Visit the sacred Temple of the Tooth Relic (Sri Dalada Maligawa).

Kandy City Tour visiting Kandy Lake Drive, Gem Museum, Silk & Batik factory. Drive to Nuwara Eliya: visit Sri Baktha Hanuman Temple in Ramboda (featuring 16ft tall Hanuman statue), Ramboda Falls (358 ft high waterfall), Ceylon Tea Factory & Plantation tour with tea tasting, and evening stroll at Gregory Lake. Dinner and overnight stay at Asford Hotel Nuwara Eliya.

Day 3 · 18 Oct 2026
Nuwara Eliya to Bentota
Seetha Amman Temple & Herbal Garden

Breakfast at hotel. Visit Seetha Amman Temple (Ashok Vatika Ramayana site).

Visit Regent Spice & Herbal Garden to learn about medicinal herbs, Sandalwood, Nutmeg, Cinnamon & Saffron. Drive down to coastal paradise Bentota. Enjoy evening water sports on Bentota River/Beach. Dinner and overnight stay at Laluna Ayurveda Resort / Club Bentota.

Day 4 · 19 Oct 2026
Bentota to Colombo
Madu River Safari & Turtle Hatchery

Breakfast at hotel. Boat Safari along Madu River Estuary.

Glide across 64 mangrove islands of Madu River, visit Ma Doowa island and Satha Paha Doowa shrine. Visit Kosgoda Sea Turtle Conservation Project & Hatchery to see baby sea turtles. Transfer to Colombo for City Tour & Shopping: Galle Face Green, Old Parliament, Dutch Hospital, Viharamahadevi Park, Independence Square & Lotus Tower. Dinner and overnight stay at Berjaya Hotel Colombo.

Day 5 · 20 Oct 2026
Colombo Airport Departure
CMB Transfer

Breakfast at hotel. Check-out from hotel.

Private vehicle transfer from Colombo hotel to Bandaranaike International Airport for your departure flight with beautiful memories of Sri Lanka.
