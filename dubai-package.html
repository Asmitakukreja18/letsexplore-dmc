5 Days Detailed Itinerary

Day-by-Day Experience

Day 1 · 16 Oct 2026
DXB Arrival & Dhow Cruise
Dubai Marina Dinner Cruise

Arrive at Dubai International Airport (DXB). Private transfer to Grand Excelsior Hotel Bur Dubai (4★).

Check-in and relax. In the evening, enjoy a romantic Dubai Marina Dhow Cruise Dinner featuring a lavish international buffet, tanoura dance show, and stunning views of Dubai Marina skyscrapers. Overnight stay in Dubai.

Day 2 · 17 Oct 2026
Dubai City Tour & Burj Khalifa
124th Floor Summit & Dubai Mall

Breakfast at the hotel. 4-Hour Guided Dubai City Tour.

Drive through Jumeirah Mosque, Etihad Museum, Zabeel Palace photo stop, visit Gold Souk & Spice Souk, explore Al Bastakiya historic area, Burj Al Arab info & photo stop, and Atlantis The Palm photo stop. Photo stops at Dubai Frame and Museum of the Future. In the evening, visit Burj Khalifa 124th Floor Observation Deck (Non-Prime Hours) with access to Dubai Mall and Dubai Fountain Light Show. Overnight stay in Dubai.

Day 3 · 18 Oct 2026
Desert Safari Standard PVT
4x4 Dune Bashing & BBQ Dinner

Breakfast at the hotel. Morning free for self exploration or shopping.

In the afternoon, pickup by private 4x4 Land Cruiser for Desert Safari. Thrilling dune bashing in Lahbab red dunes, camel riding, sandboarding, henna painting, Arabic coffee & dates welcome. Enjoy live belly dance, tanoura show, fire show, and a delicious BBQ buffet dinner under the desert stars. Overnight stay in Dubai.

Day 4 · 19 Oct 2026
Abu Dhabi City Tour PVT
Grand Mosque, BAPS Temple & Ferrari World

Breakfast at the hotel. Full day private Abu Dhabi City Tour.

Visit the magnificent BAPS Hindu Mandir Abu Dhabi, Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, Abu Dhabi Corniche, Emirates Palace photo stop, and photo stop at Ferrari World Abu Dhabi. Return to Dubai in the evening. Overnight stay in Dubai.

Day 5 · 20 Oct 2026
Dubai Airport Departure
DXB Transfer

Breakfast at the hotel. Check-out from hotel.

Private vehicle transfer from Bur Dubai hotel to DXB Airport for your return flight home with unforgettable memories of Dubai.
