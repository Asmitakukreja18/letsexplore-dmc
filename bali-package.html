7 Days Detailed Itinerary

Day-by-Day Experience

Day 1 · 01 Mar 2026
Denpasar Airport & Finns Beach Club
Welcome Flower Garland

Arrive at Ngurah Rai Denpasar Airport (DPS). Traditional flower garland welcome by driver-guide.

Private vehicle transfer to Kuta hotel. Check-in and relax. In the evening, head out for a vibrant sunset vibe at Finns Beach Club Canggu / Seminyak. Overnight stay in Kuta.

Day 2 · 02 Mar 2026
Tanjung Benoa & Uluwatu Kecak
Banana Boat, Parasailing, Jet Ski & Padang Padang

Breakfast at hotel. Full day private vehicle tour of South Bali.

Enjoy Watersports Package A at Tanjung Benoa (Banana Boat ride, Single Parasailing & Jet Ski with instructor). Visit picturesque Padang Padang Beach (Eat Pray Love filming site). Evening visit to Pura Uluwatu Cliff Temple with admission tickets included for the dramatic sunset Kecak & Fire Dance performance. Overnight stay in Kuta.

Day 3 · 03 Mar 2026
Full Day West Nusa Penida Tour
Fast Boat Ferry, Kelingking T-Rex & Snorkeling

Early morning transfer from hotel to Sanur Harbour. Fast boat ferry across to Nusa Penida Island.

Explore West Nusa Penida: iconic Kelingking T-Rex Cliff view point, Broken Beach (Pasih Uug), Angel's Billabong natural infinity pool, and Crystal Bay. Enjoy local Indonesian lunch and complimentary beachside snorkeling (gear provided). Fast boat return to Sanur harbour and transfer back to hotel. Overnight stay in Kuta.

Day 4 · 04 Mar 2026
Bedugul & Tanah Lot Sea Temple
Handara Gate & Lake Beratan Temple

Breakfast at hotel. Full day Bedugul Zone private vehicle tour.

Visit the famous Handara Golf Course Iconic Gate for photos, Pura Ulun Danu Beratan (floating lake temple on Lake Beratan), and sunset photo stop at the dramatic ocean temple Pura Tanah Lot. Overnight stay in Kuta / Ubud.

Day 5 · 05 Mar 2026
Ayung River Rafting & ATV Biking
Buffet Lunch & Tegenungan Waterfall

Breakfast at hotel. White Water Rafting on Ayung River with professional safety guides.

Enjoy Indonesian buffet lunch at rafting finish point. Experience 90-120 minute ATV Quad Biking at Jambe Asri (tandem quad bike ride through jungle trails, mud pools & rice fields, safety gear & insurance included). Visit lush Tegenungan Waterfall. Overnight stay in Ubud.

Day 6 · 06 Mar 2026
Kintamani Volcano & Desa Swings
Coffee Plantation & Ubud Art Market

Breakfast at hotel. Full day Kintamani Zone private vehicle tour.

Desa Swing Package 2 (Unlimited 7 Swings + 5 Nests: Extreme River Swing, Sun Rise Swing, Romantic Couple Swing & Bird Nests). Visit Tegalalang Rice Terraces, Bas De Atanaya Coffee Plantation for free coffee/tea tasting tour, Kintamani Mount Batur Volcano view point, and walking tour of Ubud Royal Palace & Ubud Traditional Art Market. Overnight stay in Ubud.

Day 7 · 07 Mar 2026
Ubud to Denpasar Airport
DPS Airport Drop

Breakfast at hotel. Check-out from Ubud hotel.

Private vehicle airport drop transfer from Ubud Central to Ngurah Rai Denpasar Airport (DPS) for departure flight.
