5 Days Detailed Itinerary

Day-by-Day Experience

Day 1 · 12 Jan 2026
Chandigarh to Manali Scenic Drive
Beas River Terrains & Check-in

Arrival at Chandigarh Airport or Railway Station. Meet representative and start drive towards Manali.

Enjoy cool mountain breeze along the winding Beas River valley with breathtaking pine forest views. Check in at hotel in Manali. Relax and enjoy dinner. Overnight stay in Manali.

Day 2 · 13 Jan 2026
Manali Local City Tour
Hadimba Temple, Ghatotkacha & Clubhouse

Breakfast at hotel. Full day Manali local sightseeing tour.

Visit 16th-century wooden Hadimba Devi Temple set inside Dhungri Van Vihar, Ghatotkacha Temple, Vashisht Hot Sulphur Springs, Clubhouse (skating, disco, zipline, TT), Tibetan Monastery, and evening stroll at Mall Road. Dinner and overnight stay in Manali.

Day 3 · 14 Jan 2026
Solang Valley Adventure Excursion
Paragliding, Zorbing, ATV Rides & Snow Sports

Breakfast at hotel. Full day excursion to Solang Valley.

Solang Valley is Himachal's premier adventure sports destination. Indulge in thrill activities like Paragliding, Zorbing, ATV Quad bike rides, trail riding, and snow play. Return to hotel in evening for dinner and comfortable stay in Manali.

Day 4 · 15 Jan 2026
Naggar Castle & Kullu Valley Excursion
Roerich Art Gallery & Kullu Shawls

Breakfast at hotel. Day trip to historic Naggar Town & Kullu Valley.

Explore Naggar Castle (historic abode of Kullu Rajas and movie shooting hotspot), Nicholas Roerich Art Gallery (original paintings of famous Russian painter), and visit Kullu Shawl Weaving Factory & Angora rabbit farms. Return to hotel for dinner and overnight stay in Manali.

Day 5 · 16 Jan 2026
Manali to Chandigarh Departure
IXC Airport / Station Drop

Breakfast at hotel. Check-out from hotel.

Souvenir shopping at local market. Private vehicle return drive back to Chandigarh Airport or Railway Station for onward journey filled with Himalayan memories.
