5 Days Detailed Itinerary

Day-by-Day Experience

Day 1 · 16 Oct 2026
Changi Arrival & Night Safari
World's First Nocturnal Zoo

Arrive at Singapore Changi Airport. Private Combi Van Airport transfer to Hotel Boss (4★ Premium).

Check-in and relax. In the evening, private Combi transfer for Night Safari Tour. Guided tram ride through 6 geographical zones of nocturnal wildlife, Creatures of the Night show, and walking trails. Return transfer to hotel. Overnight stay in Singapore.

Day 2 · 17 Oct 2026
City Tour & Sentosa Island
Cable Car, Madame Tussauds & Wings of Time

Breakfast at hotel. Guided City Orientation Tour.

Drive through CBD Area, Chinatown photo stop, Civic District, Esplanade Theatre by the Bay, Helix Bridge, Marina Floating Bay, Merlion Park photo stop, and Suntec City. Afternoon Sentosa Island tour: Mount Faber Cable Car ride, Madame Tussauds 4-in-1 (Images of Singapore Live, Spirit of Singapore Boat Ride, Ultimate Filmstar Experience), and spectacular Wings of Time Fireworks Symphony (1st Show). Private Combi transfers included. Overnight stay in Singapore.

Day 3 · 18 Oct 2026
Universal Studios Singapore
Full Day Theme Park Thrills

Breakfast at hotel. Private Combi transfer to Resort World Sentosa.

Full day admission ticket to Universal Studios Singapore (USS). Experience Hollywood Walk of Fame, Sci-Fi City (Transformers 3D Ride), Ancient Egypt (Revenge of the Mummy), Jurassic Park Rapids Adventure, Far Far Away (Shrek 4D Adventure), and WaterWorld stunt show. Private Combi return transfer to hotel. Overnight stay in Singapore.

Day 4 · 19 Oct 2026
MBS Skypark & Gardens by the Bay
Flower Dome, Cloud Forest & Supertrees

Breakfast at hotel. Private Combi transfers to Marina Bay.

Visit Marina Bay Sands (MBS) SkyPark Observation Deck (57th floor panoramic skyline views). Explore Gardens by the Bay including Flower Dome, Cloud Forest (Jurassic World experience with indoor mist waterfall), and evening Supertree Grove Garden Rhapsody Light Show. Overnight stay in Singapore.

Day 5 · 20 Oct 2026
Singapore Changi Departure
Changi Airport Transfer

Breakfast at hotel. Check-out from hotel.

Private Combi Van transfer to Singapore Changi Airport (jewel waterfall tour optional) for your return flight home.
