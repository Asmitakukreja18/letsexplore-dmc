10 Days Detailed Itinerary

Day-by-Day Experience

Day 1 · 23 Nov 2026
Kathmandu Arrival & Pashupatinath

Arrival at Kathmandu Tribhuvan Airport. Private transfer to hotel. Evening attend holy Aarati ceremony at Pashupatinath Temple on Bagmati river. Dinner & overnight in Kathmandu.

Day 2 · 24 Nov 2026
Kathmandu City Sightseeing

Visit Jal Narayan Temple (Budhanilkantha Sleeping Vishnu), Boudhanath Stupa (UNESCO World Heritage), Swoyambhunath Stupa (Monkey Temple) & Kathmandu Durbar Square (Living Goddess Kumari Temple). Dinner & overnight in Kathmandu.

Day 3 · 25 Nov 2026
Kathmandu to Pokhara via Manakamana

Drive to Pokhara (210 km, 8-9 hrs). En-route visit Manakamana Temple by Cable Car. Evening stroll along Lakeside Pokhara night market. Overnight in Pokhara.

Day 4 · 26 Nov 2026
Pumdikot Sunrise & Pokhara Tour

Early 5 AM drive to Pumdikot Shiva Temple for Annapurna sunrise view. Visit Davis Waterfalls, Gupteshwor Mahadev Cave, Bindabasini Temple, Seti River Gorge, and 1-hr boat ride on Phewa Lake to Tal Barahi Temple. Overnight in Pokhara.

Day 5 · 27 Nov 2026
Pokhara to Jomsom 4x4 Drive

Scorpio 4x4 drive to Jomsom via Tatopani Hot Springs & Rupse Waterfalls (8-9 hrs drive). Check-in at Jomsom hotel. Dinner & overnight in Jomsom.

Day 6 · 28 Nov 2026
Muktinath Darshan (3,800m) & Pokhara

Early morning 4x4 drive to Ranipauwa (3,710m), walk 25 mins to sacred Muktinath Temple (3,800m). Perform puja, holy bath at 108 spouts. Return drive via Jomsom back to Pokhara (10-11 hrs). Overnight in Pokhara.

Day 7 · 29 Nov 2026
Pokhara to Chitwan National Park

Drive to Chitwan (150 km, 5 hrs). Check-in at Green Mansion Resort & lunch. Afternoon Tharu Village tour, sunset point, and Tharu Cultural Stick Dance show. Full board meals. Overnight in Chitwan.

Day 8 · 30 Nov 2026
Chitwan Jungle Safari Activities

Canoe ride on Rapti River (Crocodile watching), Elephant Breeding Center & Jungle Walk. Afternoon Jeep Safari inside Chitwan National Park (one-horned rhino & tiger spotting). Full board meals. Overnight in Chitwan.

Day 9 · 01 Dec 2026
Bhaktapur Durbar & Nagarkot Sunset

Drive to Nagarkot via Bhaktapur Durbar Square (UNESCO site, 55 Window Palace). Check-in at Hotel Himalayan Villa Nagarkot. Watch sunset over Himalayas from hotel balcony. Dinner & overnight in Nagarkot.

Day 10 · 02 Dec 2026
Himalayan Sunrise & Departure

Watch morning sunrise over Mt. Everest range from room balcony. Breakfast, check-out, private transfer to Kathmandu Airport for return flight.
